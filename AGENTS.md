# Project instructions for AI coding agents

Read [docs/PROJECT_GOALS_SEO.md](docs/PROJECT_GOALS_SEO.md) before planning any changes.

## Non-negotiable product objective

Sweet-lana / Lana Sweet Perfect is a **brand awareness and Google organic search visibility project** for cosmetologist Svetlana Petrova in Riga, Latvia. Its primary outcomes are more relevant impressions, ranking coverage, organic clicks, and website visits from Google. Booking enquiries and future skincare sales are secondary, not a replacement for the acquisition goal.

## Current phase: SEO-ready development, NOT active promotion

The owner explicitly confirmed that **active SEO promotion is not being launched yet**. All new pages, content, navigation and components must be built SEO-ready so the later campaign does not require architectural rework. Do not initiate outreach, ranking campaigns, paid ads, Search Console submission, analytics setup, or new marketing integrations without a separate request. SEO technical foundations may be retained, but technical indexability is not a claim that a promotion campaign has launched.

## Implementation guardrails

- Preserve crawlable, indexable public landing pages, working robots.txt/sitemap, and canonical/hreflang. Do not add global noindex.
- Maintain three real language versions (LV/RU/EN) with unique metadata and localized SEO copy; avoid publishing machine-stuffed keyword lists.
- Keep dedicated search-relevant pages for Imanta (Dammes iela 46A) and Liderm (Hanzas iela 18), with their actual services and local details. Do not merge all services onto one generic page.
- Prefer genuinely useful content by customer concern/treatment, with accurate medical phrasing, credible qualifications, and real images; never invent claims, customer testimonials, costs, certifications, or reviews.
- Preserve mobile UX, performance, accessibility, internal links and visitor clarity.
- Keep unpublished content, admin, login and APIs out of search. Verify live publication on Railway, not just GitHub commits.
- When promotion is explicitly approved later, measure growth using Search Console queries, indexed pages, organic CTR/clicks and organic landing-page visits; avoid unverified promises of ranking #1.

When adding features, explicitly consider how they affect Google discovery, trust, and organic traffic. See README and project SEO strategy for details.
